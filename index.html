Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay the KSh 4,500 deposit? ▾

Go to M-Pesa → Send Money → Enter 0758063552 → Enter KSh 4,500. Once sent, forward your confirmation text via WhatsApp to the same number (0758063552) with your business name to begin work immediately.

When is the KSh 4,000 balance paid? ▾

We build your complete working website on a private preview link. You review it on your smartphone. Only when you are completely satisfied with the design and layout do you pay the remaining KSh 4,000 balance before we connect your final domain.

Do I really own 100% of the code? ▾

Yes. You receive original source code files and your own GitHub repository. You are never locked in and can transfer or deploy your site to any host in the world whenever you wish.

What are the ongoing costs from Year 2 onwards? ▾

Your 1st-year domain is covered. From Year 2 onwards, annual domain renewal (.co.ke or .com) and ongoing Cloudflare DNS management runs between KSh 3,000 and KSh 5,000 per year. There are zero mandatory monthly agency fees.
